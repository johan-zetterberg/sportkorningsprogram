\# Contributing



Thanks for your interest in improving Sportkörningsprogram!



\## Guiding principles

\- Keep changes small and reviewable.

\- Don’t add third-party copyrighted or trademarked assets (e.g., federation PDFs, official logos) to the repo.

\- Prefer clear, robust solutions over “quick hacks”.



\## How to contribute

1\. Fork the repository (or request collaborator access if agreed).

2\. Create a feature branch:

&nbsp;  - `feature/<short-description>` or `fix/<short-description>`

3\. Make your changes with clear commits.

4\. Open a Pull Request (PR) describing:

&nbsp;  - What changed

&nbsp;  - Why

&nbsp;  - How to test



\## Contribution license / rights

By submitting a contribution (code, documentation, or other content) to this repository, you agree that:

\- You have the right to submit the contribution.

\- Your contribution is provided to Johan Zetterberg for inclusion in the project.

\- Johan Zetterberg may use, modify, relicense, and distribute your contribution as part of this project.



If you cannot agree to this, please do not submit a contribution.



\## Reporting issues

Use GitHub Issues and include:

\- Steps to reproduce

\- Expected vs actual behavior

\- Screenshots/logs if relevant
